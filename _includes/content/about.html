Hi, I’m Elliott.

Physics was my first love. Studying it taught me to ask what we know, what we’re assuming, and what we can test. Those questions still guide my work as a machine learning engineer. I find meaning in figuring out how data can help people make decisions that matter in their lives.

These days I build tools for technology scouting. Earlier, I trained models to spot objects in satellite images and made them run faster on hardware designed to mimic a satellite’s onboard computer. I also worked with radio signals, developing an algorithm to reduce interference between transmissions.

I do independent research because I love spending time with questions that fascinate me. One I keep coming back to is how a neural network with so many parameters can make good predictions on data it’s never seen. Lately I’ve been testing how much a network’s weights can tell us about how it will behave on new inputs.
