## About me

I’m **Elliott C. Miller**, a Machine Learning Engineer with a background in physics (BS, RIT) and nearly a decade of experience building production CV and NLP systems on satellite imagery and RF data. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}  

My current focus is **LLM systems, generalization, and GPU-accelerated AI**:

- At **Cylitix (Senior Lead AI Engineer)** I design LLM-based agents on top of Amazon Bedrock and Hugging Face, using LangChain and LangGraph to ship RAG and routing systems for government tech scouting. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}  
- Previously at **BlackSky (Machine Learning Engineer)** I built CV and NLP models for satellite imagery and social data, deployed them to AWS, and optimized models to run on satellite-emulating hardware with large speed and size improvements. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}  
- At **Digital Global Systems** I automated RF IQ data demodulation and built analytics around wireless spectrum deconfliction using Gaussian mixtures and graph theory. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}  

I’m especially interested in:

- **GPU & systems work** – CUDA/TensorRT, efficient inference, and scaling training on multi-GPU setups. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}  
- **LLM generalization** – weight-space learning, LoRA/QLoRA, and using margin-based diagnostics to understand model behavior.
- **Neural implicit representations / NeRF-style methods** and their connection to generalization and compression. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}
